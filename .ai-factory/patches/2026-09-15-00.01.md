# Importer retained the removed Garmin garth interface

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Files:** `garmin_import.py`, `test_garmin_import.py`
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

Deleting the original activity failed with a missing `garth` attribute, and
uploading the replacement then returned a duplicate activity error.

## Root Cause

The importer still used the old authentication transport after the dependency
upgrade. Deletion, renaming, and fresh-session persistence were affected.

## Solution

Use the public `delete_activity` and `set_activity_name` methods and persist
sessions with `client.client.dump`. Offline deletion and rename regressions
failed before the fix and passed afterward. Fresh-login persistence is covered
with mocked login and storage; no remote activities were changed during checks.

## Prevention

Audit all authentication backend references when upgrading Garmin dependencies.
Prefer public activity methods. Additional tests could cover failed deletion
and retention of local files after upload failure.
